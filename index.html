D · 004 · The energy loop

Trash in. Sovereign compute out. Carbon credits banked.

Municipal solid waste enters the WTE container as feedstock the community already pays to landfill. The conversion stage produces electricity sized to a ~50 GPU compute envelope, with thermal output captured by an absorption chiller that returns cold water to the compute cold aisle. Grid export carries the surplus.

The feedstock is renewable in the sense that municipalities always generate trash. The carbon profile is net-negative through landfill diversion plus avoided fossil generation. The community gets paid twice: tipping fees coming in, AI services going out.

E · 005 · Why this beats hyperscale

Crusoe uses oil-and-gas flare gas. We use the landfill.

Crusoe's thesis depends on natural gas waste from oil and gas operations, which locks them to fossil regions and fossil exposure. The Sovereign Node uses municipal solid waste from landfills surrounding tribal and rural communities. Same category, structurally superior feedstock, structurally superior positioning.

Hyperscalers are looking up at orbital data centers. We are looking down at the communities they cannot reach. Every node trains on real-time local data the network preserves rather than averages away.

A two-sovereign partnership

Sovereign Shield Technologies brings the sovereign compute architecture: USPTO-filed Sovereign Prompt Shield, AES-256-GCM on-device encryption, append-only audit logs, the governance framework (AILT), and eleven live deployments across health, language, education, and leadership verticals. The data layer is built.

The waste-to-energy integration is brought to the partnership by an established OEM relationship held by our Cherokee Nation counterpart. The energy layer comes through the relationship, not around it. This document specifies the reference architecture in technology-agnostic terms so that the partnership conversation determines the specific conversion technology, the carbon-credit profile, and the route to permitted commercial operation.

This is the structural form of nation-to-nation collaboration we are building toward: a Chickasaw-founded sovereign AI infrastructure company integrated with a Cherokee-routed waste-to-energy technology stack, deployed on tribal land, serving communities the hyperscalers cannot reach.
